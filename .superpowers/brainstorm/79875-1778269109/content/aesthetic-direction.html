A. Terminal Hacker

Dark background, green/cyan/magenta terminal text. ASCII art rendered as if you're in a hacking terminal. Random characters rain down the screen. Navigation feels like typing commands. Think: The Matrix meets a BBS.

B. Cosmic Void

Deep purple/black gradients with floating ASCII constellations. Art morphs and drifts slowly. Soft glows and particle effects. Navigation emerges from the void. Think: A planetarium made of characters.

C. Glitch Gallery

Clean dark background with bold colored ASCII art that glitches, scrambles, and reforms. Scanlines, CRT effects, random character substitution. Each page load generates unique art. Think: A corrupted art gallery that's beautiful in its chaos.

You can also mix elements — e.g., the cosmic palette with the glitch effects. Click what resonates most.
