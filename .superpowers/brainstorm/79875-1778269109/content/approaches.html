A. The Dashboard

Your site IS a dashboard. The landing page shows your video hero, then immediately presents your work as a live data product — each section shows real GA4 analytics inline. Websites show traffic bars, Art shows a visual grid, Blog shows a timeline. A live stats ticker runs across the top. Everything feels like a command center for your creative output.

Strengths

  • Unique — nobody does this
  • Shows your analytics/data chops immediately
  • Live data makes it feel alive
  • Single page, no navigation friction

Trade-offs

  • More backend work (GA4 per-project)
  • Could feel dense on mobile

B. The Cinematic Portal

Dramatic, editorial feel. Full-bleed video hero with your name in elegant thin type, then three portal cards that glow with their section color. Each portal leads to its own full page. Analytics live inside each section page, not on the landing. The landing page is pure vibes — the data lives one click deeper.

Strengths

  • Cinematic first impression
  • Clean, not overwhelming
  • Each section gets its own world
  • Easier to maintain/extend

Trade-offs

  • Extra click to see content
  • Less "wow I can see everything" factor

My recommendation: A (The Dashboard). It's bolder, more unique, and the live analytics immediately differentiate you from every other portfolio. The cyberpunk data-product aesthetic comes through strongest when the data is front and center. We can make it work on mobile with a stacked layout.
