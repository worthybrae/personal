Visual Direction: Generative Binary Art

Which of these feels closest to the vibe you're going for? These are rough concepts — we'd refine whichever direction you pick.

A — Binary Rain → Image Reveal

Streams of 0s and 1s cascade down and gradually coalesce into recognizable images of your projects. Think Matrix-meets-data-art. The digits are the medium — they form, dissolve, reform into the next project. Looping animation.

B — Binary Pointillism

Each pixel of your project screenshots is replaced by a tiny 0 or 1, sized and colored to reconstruct the image. On hover or scroll, the digits scatter and reassemble into the next project. More static/controlled than the rain — like a data visualization of your work.

C — Binary Topography

0s and 1s arranged in contour-line patterns that suggest depth and landscape — like topographic maps made of data. Projects emerge from these contour fields. More abstract and artistic, less literal image reconstruction. Feels like data visualization art.

D — Binary Mask Over Video

Your existing project videos play underneath, but rendered through a binary character grid. The digits shift to match brightness — bright areas get 1s, dark areas get 0s. Real video content is visible but filtered through the "code lens." Keeps your existing media while adding the generative layer.
